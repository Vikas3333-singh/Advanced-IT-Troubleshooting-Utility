Phase 1 — The Problem Statement

Manual, Inconsistent & Unscalable Fleet Maintenance

Prior to standardization, IT support engineers were handling routine system health issues, OS corruption, and performance degradation manually on every device.

High Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR)

A standard ticket involving disk repair, cache purges, SFC/DISM scans, and service optimization required 35–45 minutes of active, manual engineer intervention per laptop, creating massive ticket backlogs.

Average 40 Minutes Wasted per Incident

Disjointed Windows vs. macOS Standard

The support team operated with zero standardized playbook across operating systems. Windows troubleshooting was ad-hoc via GUI menus, while macOS endpoints were frequently escalated due to lack of deep shell tooling.

Inconsistent SLAs Across Operating Systems

Inability to Automate via Intune / SCCM

Maintenance scripts lacked silent CLI argument capabilities, dry-run safety modes, and atomic process locking. This made silent push via enterprise MDM tools risky and impossible to run at scale.

0% Silent Deployment Capability

Zero Audit Trails & Root-Cause Diagnostics

Support engineers performed fixes without generating persistent local logs or telemetry. IT management had no visibility into recurring hardware degradation, battery wear, or recurring corrupted OS binaries.

No Diagnostic Retention or Telemetry

Phase 2 — Engineering & Execution

Architecting the Automated Endpoint Utility

Engineered an enterprise-ready, dual-OS automation suite designed for unattended L1/L2 support, remote silent deployment, and robust system integrity.

Cross-Platform Native Script Engines

Developed windows_maintenance_final.bat (Windows 10/11) and maintenance.sh with strict POSIX standards (set -Eeuo pipefail) on macOS Ventura/Sonoma/Sequoia.

Silent Headless MDM Integration (Intune & SCCM)

Engineered 15+ CLI arguments (e.g. --silent, --auto, --sfc, --cleanup) enabling enterprise distribution without popping up disruptive windows on end-user screens.

Fail-Safe Mechanisms & Dry-Run Engine

Integrated atomic process locks (PID validation to stop duplicate execution), automated disk space pre-flight checks, and non-destructive --dry-run modes to safeguard production machines.

Natural Language Issue Triage Router

Engineered an intelligent routing subsystem on Windows where L1 technicians can type queries in plain conversational English (e.g., "slow network", "office crashing"), which auto-routes directly to the right troubleshooting module.

Phase 4 — Strategic Business Value

Executive & Organizational Benefits

How this engineering project translated technical scripting into executive ROI, compliance safety, and enterprise productivity.

Direct Support Cost Reduction

By saving ~35 minutes per device maintenance ticket across hundreds of endpoints, the IT department recovered hundreds of engineering hours annually, cutting cost-per-ticket dramatically.

Financial & Resource ROI

Zero Downtime for End Users

Executives, consultants, and staff are no longer forced to give up their laptops for hours during business calls. Maintenance runs silently in the background or during off-hours through MDM.

Employee Productivity

Audit & Compliance Readiness

Structured JSON health telemetry, battery wear rates, and OS integrity checks are stored locally and are ready for SIEM ingestion, empowering IT leadership with actionable fleet intelligence.

Governance & Security

Elimination of Human Error

Eliminated accidental deletions or misconfigured registry settings caused by manual technician intervention. Deterministic scripts execute identical logic on every single device.

Operational Stability

Accelerated L1 Onboarding

New support engineers do not require weeks of training on complex terminal commands or manual registry tweaks. The natural language router and unified script allow immediate productivity.

Team Scalability

Standardized SLA Across OS Fleets

Bridged the historical operational gap between Windows and macOS endpoint support, unifying both under a singular corporate service-level agreement.
